## Overview

In this project, I explored the use of device-free sensing technology, specifically Wi-Fi Channel State Information (CSI), for unobtrusive monitoring of daily life activities and behaviour of older adults in their everyday environments. The research brought together pervasive computing, artificial intelligence, behavioural science, and human-centered design to investigate how sensing technologies can provide meaningful behavioural insights while minimizing the burden on users.
